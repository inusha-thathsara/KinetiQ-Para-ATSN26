# Media & Test Footage Attribution (ATSN'26 KinetiQ Para)

All test footage used during development and demonstration follows strict licensing and consent ethics. Raw video clips live in `data/clips/` (git-ignored) and are **not** committed to the public repository.

## 1. Built-In Seeded Demo Clips
- **Source:** Programmatically generated by `apps/api/app/cv/pose.py` (`generate_synthetic_demo_clip`) using OpenCV + NumPy + ffmpeg (H.264).
- **License:** Project-owned (MIT / ATSN'26 prototype).
- **Use:** Instant 1-click live demo fallback (`wheelchair_propulsion`, `standing_para_gait`, `guide_runner_sync`) with zero identifiable personal data.

## 3. Consented Club Footage (Recommended Before Final Dec 7 Video)
- Teammates reaching out to a local university or Paralympic track club should obtain written/recorded athlete consent before filming a 10–15 second side-view clip (`3–5 m` perpendicular to the lane) and log the consent date here.
- **Never** scrape or reuse identifiable Para-athlete broadcast footage without explicit permission.
